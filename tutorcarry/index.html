Support

Contact Eric Wu at ericwu.sh021@gmail.com for help with TutorCarry. Include your app version, iOS version and a description of the problem. Please leave learner names, lesson photos and private notes out of support requests.

Make a parent update

Start a lesson, enter a learner nickname, and write what you covered. You can choose a photo and select recognized text to add to your notes. Review the update and confirm its accuracy before saving. Open the saved lesson to share its parent update through the iOS share sheet.

Protect your notebook

Open Settings → Prepare a backup, then save the file somewhere you trust. A backup contains your full notebook, including photos and private notes. Restoring a backup replaces your current notebook. Export a current backup first if you want to keep it.

Photo text is imperfect

Text recognition runs on your device and works best with clear, well-lit text. Check recognized lines before including them in a parent update. TutorCarry organizes the information you supply; it does not assess learners or infer their progress from a photo.

Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026. TutorCarry is provided by Eric Wu.

Lesson content stays on your device

TutorCarry stores lesson records, photographs, recognized text and drafts locally on your device. Text recognition uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. We do not operate a server that receives lesson content. The app has no account system, advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK.

You control sharing

The parent text update includes the learner name or nickname, lesson date, subject, what you covered, your observation and practice notes. It excludes private notes, next-session notes, source photos and recognized source text unless you intentionally copy that text into a parent-facing field.

Backup files contain the full notebook, including private content. When you send a parent update or save a backup to another app or cloud service, that destination handles the information according to its own policies. Opening the share sheet does not mean a message was delivered.

Apple services

Apple processes purchases. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may provide aggregate sales information, diagnostics and opt-in App Analytics according to your Apple settings and its privacy policies. Your device backup may include the notebook according to your system settings.

Support, retention and deletion

If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use that information to address your request and retain it only as needed for support and applicable obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by email.

You can delete individual lesson records in the app. Deleting TutorCarry can remove its local data; export a backup first if you need it. Copies you exported and device backups are managed separately by you and the relevant service. We cannot retrieve a notebook that only existed on your device.

Learner information

TutorCarry is intended for tutors managing their own notes. Use a nickname or initials when possible. Only capture and share material you have permission to use. Learners do not create accounts or submit content to us through the app.

For privacy questions, contact ericwu.sh021@gmail.com. Material changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

Free lessons and Pro

Five completed lesson records are free. Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase that unlocks unlimited saved lesson records. There is no subscription. The App Store displays the current localized price before purchase.

Photo text recognition, private notes and parent updates are included in Free. Existing records remain readable, editable and shareable after the free allowance is used. Deleting a record does not reset the allowance.

Use Restore purchases with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Purchase restoration unlocks Pro; it does not recover your notebook. Refund requests are handled by Apple.
